🔨 Build & Flash

Compile Korvo firmware on this Mac and flash over USB. Pick the serial port that matches your board.

Flash only — idf.py build does not use the port. If you have two USB-UART cables, choose the one on the Korvo you are flashing (the list prefers usbserial over SLAB).

Runs on this Mac (same as a local terminal). If output hangs or stays empty, open /api/flash/diag — idf_py must be non-empty.
